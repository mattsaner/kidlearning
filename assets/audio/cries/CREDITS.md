# Animal sound credits

The animal cries are real recordings from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/).
They were converted to mono MP3, trimmed to about 3 seconds, loudness-normalised and faded out; nothing else was changed
(the pig grunt is repeated twice; the fish "blub" is a bubble sound played three times at different pitches, as fish are silent).
The recordings keep their original licenses, which are different from the MIT license of the code.
